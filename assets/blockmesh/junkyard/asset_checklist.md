# Junkyard concept asset checklist

## Ready

- Blue sedan and dark burgundy hatchback (in the adjacent cars pack).
- One loose car tire and one arranged twelve-tire heap.
- Two open scrap-container proportions: tall bin and long skip.
- Mixed scrap heap with engine shapes.
- Tracked sorting crane with hanging grapple.
- Small utility cabinet for the fence edges (interpretation of small details).

## Make next, based on the reference

1. Car-storage racks: reusable steel uprights, crossbars and platforms; northwest.
2. Workshop: corrugated shed, open work bay, workbench and small exterior clutter;
   west side, above the middle of the yard.
3. Old bus: a tipped-over version for the east-centre obstacle.
4. Metal-beam bundle: parallel long beams on supports; east side below the bus.
5. Wooden pallets and a tire-storage station; southwest.
6. Perimeter fence or wall: straight sections, corner posts and support pieces.
7. Double entrance gate and gateposts; south entrance.
8. Loose engine, drum, box, appliance and container variants for perimeter clutter.
9. Filled-bin variants using small scrap piles; northeast and southeast.
10. Ground dressing: small rocks, rubble clusters, weeds, shrubs and sparse trees.
11. Ground surface: dirt, tire tracks and worn paths for the final environment pass.

Layout pass: establish entrance, perimeter and navigable routes first; then place
workshop, racks, bus and crane. Fill around them with duplicated cars, bins, tires
and scrap. The image's dimension labels do not provide a reliable consistent
scale, so judge the layout against car and character sizes before fixing yard size.
