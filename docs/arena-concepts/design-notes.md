# Arena concepts

Both arenas: 120 x 80 metres. Illustrations show placement and atmosphere, not measured construction drawings. No simulator code changed.

## Movement targets

- Main routes: 12–16 m clear, with 18–22 m turning pockets. Verify full animated leg sweep.
- Player passages: 1.5–2 m wide, short and open at both ends. Verify human clearance and Leaper reach.
- Every major island needs two broad approaches. Avoid protected sniper roofs and dead-end refuges.
- Keep decorative debris against cover, outside movement routes.

## Junkyard: The Breaker Yard

1. Northwest: car towers and crushed cubes; tall sight blockers.
2. Northeast: offset containers; hard cover with player passage.
3. West centre: diagonal tipped bus and cars; lane divider with player passage.
4. East centre: claw crane and compact scrap mound; orientation landmark.
5. Southwest: low cubes, tires and cars; hop-over cover and player passage.
6. Southeast: appliances and scrap; irregular medium cover.

An offset low centre island splits approaches. Broad curved connections support circling and pursuit. Intended low cover height: 0.7–1.1 m; cars approximately 1.4–2 m. Verify jump clearance. The illustration exaggerates car tower heights; use 2–3 cars per tower for the first blockout.

## Train yard: Broken Switch

1. Northwest: chopped locomotive cab.
2. Northeast: water tower and sleeper stacks.
3. West centre: staggered short boxcar segments with broad crossing.
4. East centre: cut tipped coach and tanker fragment.
5. Southwest: short container flat and wheel bogies.
6. Southeast: short boxcar, screen and sleepers.

Use genuinely cut 6–9 m rail sections. Tipping an intact coach does not shorten it. Offset central cover interrupts long firing lines. Flush rails and wide cross-yard connections prevent parallel corridors. Narrow internal shortcuts belong within northwest, southwest and southeast clusters. The illustration adds an extra orange arrow at the western boxcars: keep that main crossing wide enough for the Leaper.

## Validation before detailed art

Test main loops in both directions, turning, reversing, jumping, camera visibility, leg collisions and central crossings. Each island needs two usable approaches. Check that players cannot indefinitely repeat a narrow shortcut without exposure. Inspect sightlines at player height and interrupt full-field shots.

Cyan arrows show route intent, not certified navigation paths. The generated robot is an illustrative stand-in, not the exact procedural Leaper. Combat balance is untested. Start with the junkyard for freer movement and varied cover height; train yard offers more directional encounters.
