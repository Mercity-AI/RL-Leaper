# Junkyard revision 2

The owner requested less compact, mushed-together scrap and a more scattered, interesting layout. This revision supersedes the original junkyard concept. The train yard is unchanged.

## Revised placement logic

- Car racks belong in a storage area, with individual racks separated by service access.
- The crane gets a clear sorting apron and separate scrap skips.
- Containers form a workshop and storage area rather than a mound of mixed junk.
- Individual dead cars, low crushed cubes and a tipped bus are staggered through the interior at different depths and orientations.
- Tires, appliances and beams have distinct, tidy storage locations. Each cover object should remain recognisable with visible ground around it.

Keep the 120 x 80 metre footprint. Use offset routes with cross-connections rather than a single empty central boulevard or six perimeter clusters. Main routes still target 12–16 metres clear width, subject to testing the Leaper's full leg sweep. Short player passages should open at both ends.

These images are concept art rather than measured navigation diagrams. Validate turning space, sightlines, cover height and shortcut fairness in a playable blockout.
