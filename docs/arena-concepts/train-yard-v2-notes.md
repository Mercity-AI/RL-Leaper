# Train yard revision 2

The owner approved the theme but requested more disruptive paths instead of straight lanes and uniform curves. This revision supersedes the original train yard arrangement.

## Layout changes

- Distribute short railcar sections through the interior at different angles and depths.
- Interrupt the central approach using offset cover with broad bypasses on both sides.
- Create irregular three-way junctions, diagonal crossings and a short dogleg instead of smooth repeated loops.
- Retain the water tower and chopped locomotive as orientation landmarks.
- Use a railway switch, interrupted rail remnants and flush maintenance aprons to reduce the dominance of parallel track lines. Derailed or relocated cars explain off-axis cover.
- Keep individual objects separate and readable. Avoid dense scrap clusters and narrow forced slaloms.

The arena remains 120 x 80 metres. Main route targets remain 12–16 m clear, with wider turning pockets. These dimensions require verification against the animated Leaper in a playable blockout. Short player-only passages need two exits and reach/collision testing.

The concept image illustrates spatial intent, not measured navigation or proven combat balance. No simulator code changed. The original image is retained for comparison.
